    A / useful rhythm

    Reference: Edward McKnight Kauffer, drawing, 1934, V&A collection E.3770-2004. The coach inspected the museum’s linked image: segmented black figure, repeated bars and circular joints, spiral hand, and a diagonal pale plane. V&A: What was Modernism?

    • Composition: a repeated rectangular rhythm groups image, message, price, and choice into aligned modules. It translates the reference’s geometric construction without quoting its figure.
    • Hierarchy: one sans-serif family and a strong headline make the message easy to scan before choosing a size.
    • Production: a shared restrained palette, reusable spacing, and a single warm accent keep the system repeatable.

    Intended effect: offer a simple everyday decision after study, with a small accent of personality. Risk: the regular structure may still feel too generic.

    This is a prototype hypothesis, not audience research or a final identity choice. Product, headline, support, price, sizes, and action match study B.
